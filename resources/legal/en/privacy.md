# Privacy Policy

Information on the processing of personal data under Articles 13 and 14 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR).

## Data controller

{store_identity}

## Data we collect

- account data: first name, last name, email, preferred language, password (stored only in encrypted form);
- optional: mobile number and home address;
- order data: products, market and pickup date, notes, amount, status and status history;
- consent to the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Sale, with date and time;
- technical access data (IP address, browser) in the security logs.

## Purposes and legal basis

- managing registration, orders, pickups and home deliveries (performance of a contract);
- sending service messages about orders, reminders and security notices (performance of a contract and legitimate interest);
- preventing abuse and protecting the seller from repeated missed pickups (legitimate interest);
- complying with legal obligations.

## Blocked customers and encrypted fingerprints

If a customer reaches the maximum number of missed pickups, the account is blocked. To prevent a new registration with the same details we keep two separate encrypted fingerprints (one of the email address and one of the mobile number), computed with a secret key. The fingerprints cannot be turned back into the original data and are kept even if the account is deleted.

## Retention

Data is kept as long as the account exists. Deleting the account erases the personal data and the orders, except for the encrypted fingerprints described above and what the law requires us to keep.

## Recipients and service providers

To provide the service we use providers that process data on our behalf:

- website hosting and infrastructure;
- email delivery service;
- WhatsApp Business Platform (Meta), only if you choose to verify your mobile number;
- browser notification services, only if you turn on notifications.

## Your rights

You can request access, rectification, erasure, restriction and portability of your data, and you can object to the processing. You can delete your account from your personal area; for the other rights, write to the data controller. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the data protection authority of your country.
