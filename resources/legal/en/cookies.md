# Cookie Policy

This site only uses technical cookies until you make a choice. No statistics or marketing cookies are set without your consent.

## Technical cookies

- **Session**: keeps you signed in and keeps your cart while you browse;
- **XSRF-TOKEN**: protects forms from unwanted requests;
- **locale**: remembers the language you chose, for one year;
- **cookie_consent**: remembers the choice made in the banner, for one year.

## Statistics and marketing cookies

They are not used at the moment. If we introduce them in the future, the banner will ask for your consent before turning them on.

## How to change your choice

You can change your mind at any time with the "Manage cookies" button at the bottom of every page. You can also delete cookies from your browser settings.
