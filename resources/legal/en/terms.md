# Terms of Sale

Conditions that apply to the orders placed on this site with {store_identity}.

## Reservation and pickup

Products are reserved online and collected in person at the market and on the day chosen when ordering, within the opening hours of that market. Each market has a minimum notice and an order deadline: the site only shows the available dates. Out-of-season products take longer to prepare; the time is shown before you confirm.

## Payment

There is no online payment. You always pay in person, at pickup or on home delivery. The receipt or invoice is issued on the spot. For home delivery, the delivery cost shown below, if any, is added to the price of the products.

## Changes and cancellation

You can change the pickup date or cancel the order from your personal area, within the minimum notice of the market. If the market is closed (holidays or an unplanned closure) we will let you know, and you can choose another date or cancel.

## Missed pickups and account blocking

The seller records orders that are not collected. After {no_show_threshold} missed pickups the account is blocked: it can no longer place orders, and it is not possible to register again with the same email or mobile number. Open orders are cancelled. Only the seller can unblock an account.

## Home delivery

Home delivery is only available to customers enabled by the seller. Minimum order: {delivery_min}. Delivery takes place within {delivery_hours} hours after the order is ready.

Home delivery may have a cost. Current cost per order: {delivery_cost}. The seller sets the cost and may change it over time: the cost applied to your order is shown in the summary before you confirm, is added to the total of the products and does not change even if the cost changes later. You pay it on delivery together with the order. There is no delivery cost for orders collected at the market.

## Right of withdrawal

For distance contracts, EU consumer law (Directive 2011/83/EU) gives consumers the right to withdraw within 14 days of receiving the goods, with the exceptions provided by law (for example goods that deteriorate quickly or are made to measure). To exercise it, write to {store_email} before pickup or within the deadline.
